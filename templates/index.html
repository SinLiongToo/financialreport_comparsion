Financial & OpEx Dashboard User Guide

Architecture, Workflow Execution, Chart Interpretation & LLM Prompts

1. One-Click End-to-End Workflow Execution

Enter any target company (e.g. ASML, TSMC, NVDA, NXP, VSH, AMAT) or a full CompaniesMarketCap URL, choose the number of years (3 to 10), and click "Run End-to-End Workflow".

Step 1: Download Automated PDF crawling with sub-second caching.
Step 2: Parse to MD Extracts text and GitHub markdown tables.
Step 3: Extract KPIs Computes Productivity ratios (Rev/FTE, GP/FTE).
Step 4: Visualize Refreshes 6 interactive Plotly charts & tables.

2. Multi-Company Peer Comparison Mode

Switch between "Single Company Deep Dive" and "Multi-Company Peer Comparison" at the top. In comparison mode, check multiple companies to analyze cross-company Gross Margin pricing power, Human Capital Productivity ROI ($/FTE), Operating Leverage, and R&D Reinvestment Intensity side-by-side.

3. Top Switcher vs. Bottom Console (Two-Way Synchronization)

Top-Right Dropdown (Company Switcher) Use this to instantly view and toggle between already-processed companies in 0.1 seconds without re-downloading.
Bottom Console (Pipeline Console) Use this to download and process new companies or change the target history length (e.g. 10 years).

4. Visual Charts & Strategic OpEx Framework Guide

• Chart 1: The Pivot (Headcount vs GM %) — Identifies when workforce expansion plateaus and margin expansion shifts to automated operational excellence.

• Chart 2: Productivity Trio (Rev / GP / OpIncome per FTE) — Quantifies human capital efficiency and automation return on investment (ROI).

• Chart 3: Operating Leverage (OpIncome & Margins) — Evaluates fixed-cost absorption and profitability resilience across cyclical demand swings.

• Chart 4: R&D Commitment (Expense & % of Revenue) — Analyzes technological differentiation and moat reinvestment intensity.

• Chart 5: Growth Dynamics (YoY Triangulation) — Compares Revenue YoY, GP YoY, and OpIncome YoY against Headcount growth to ensure profitable expansion.

• Chart 6: Value vs. Volume Paradox — Disaggregates product mix between high-margin flagship lines and physical factory throughput volume.

5. Form 10-K vs. Form 20-F Compatibility

Foreign issuers (ASML, TSMC) submit Form 20-F (Item 3.A summary on pages 5-15), while US domestic corporations (NVIDIA, NXP, Vishay) submit Form 10-K (Item 8 financial statements on pages 35-70). The parser automatically detects and cross-scans both structures seamlessly.

6. Using Parsed Markdown with LLMs (Gemini / Claude / ChatGPT)

Select any parsed .md file in the bottom browser, click "Copy Markdown", and paste it into Gemini with fininacial_prompt.md or sale_breakdown.md for instant 16:9 executive presentation decks and pitch scripts.
