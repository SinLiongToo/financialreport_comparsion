### I. LTETTER TO SHAREHOLDERS


#### Dear Shareholders,

In 2024, the world experienced more geopolitical conflicts. With the Russo-Ukrainian war 
and tensions in the Middle East continuing to escalate, and the US-China trade tariff dispute 
showing no signs of easing, countries forming the globalized supply chain, which was once 
driven by efficiency and cost considerations, are gradually being forced to take sides. This 
presents new challenges for businesses.

Looking back at 2024, the Chinese smartphone market had finally moved past the 
prolonged inventory adjustments. However, due to weak consumer spending in the Android 
smartphone market, which was affected by China's economic slowdown and trade barriers, 
overall end demand did not rebound as strongly as the market initially expected. Fortunately, in 
the iOS smartphone supply chain, both Cellular PA and Wi-Fi PA have benefited from the strict 
quality and performance requirements set by the end customer, allowing WIN Semi’s customers 
to remain key suppliers with a significant market share. As a result, they have maintained stable 
growth in 2024, supporting WIN Semi’s Cellular and Wi-Fi businesses to outperform the 
previous year. For the Optics business, which is also primarily tied to the iOS supply chain, as 
the end customer continued to diversify its suppliers, the business faced a continued decline in 
smartphone 3D sensing contributions in 2024, as expected. However, the Infrastructure business 
rebounded from the previous year’s downturn, achieving double-digit year-on-year growth, 
driven by strong demand for infrastructure and satellite communications. In particular, gallium 
nitride (GaN) revenue delivered an annual growth rate of 30%. Overall, WIN Semi.'s operations 
in 2024 officially rebounded from the low point in the preceding year and successfully turned 
losses into profits, achieving annual revenue growth of approximately 10%.

Fluctuations in the business environment have not affected WIN Semi.'s commitment to 
ESG and corporate governance. In 2024, the Company won the 17th Taiwan Corporate 
Sustainability Award, was ranked among the top 5% TWSE/TPEx listed companies in the 
Corporate Governance Evaluation for 10 consecutive years, and was recognized as a benchmark 
enterprise in the top 10% electronics companies with market capitalization of more than NT$10 
billion. Furthermore, WIN Semi. was selected for the DJSI World Index for five consecutive 
years and included in the S&P Sustainability Yearbook for six years. The Company's 2024 
business overview and business outlook report are as follows:

#### Operating Performance

an increase of 10.24% compared to the year 2023.  2024 net profit attributable to owners of 
parent was NT$768,133 thousand, and EPS for 2024 was NT$1.81.

#### Research and Development Status

WIN Semi. invests research and development (R&D) resources based on mainstream 
market trends and customer needs. With the rise of AI data center-related applications, huge 
data traffic has driven the demand for optical communication transmission. The development 
of high-speed, high-reliability optical devices and high-power laser sources used in silicon 
photonics has become the focus of R&D. WIN Semi. is investing in the development of PD, 
VCSEL, EML, DFB laser, and CW laser technologies. The Company is also focused on 
indium phosphide (InP) optical modulators and photonic integrated circuits to meet the 
current demand for 100G and 200G high-speed transmissions, as well as future requirements 
for 400G, 800G, and even 1.6T. In addition, with low-orbit satellites being continuously 
launched and updated every year, WIN Semi. is continuously adopting new frequency band 
technologies to meet the needs of terminal applications. For example, to meet data 
transmission requirements between satellites and gateways, the Company has expanded 
beyond the existing Ka- and Ku-bands to include V-band and E-band, which present new 
business opportunities. WIN Semi.’s development of 0.12μm and 0.1μm GaN HEMT 
technology also effectively addresses the high linearity requirements for 30 to 50GHz 
satellite communication bands and the high-power efficiency demands for 70 to 80GHz 
transmissions. The above R&D investments represent a response for the anticipated short- 
and medium-term opportunities and growth momentum for the future.


#### B. Business Plan in 2025

According to statistics from research institutions, global smartphone shipments grew by 
a single-digit percentage in 2024 as previously expected, and is forecast to grow at a similar 
level in 2025. WIN Semi. achieved a 10% annual revenue growth in 2024. Barring external 
geopolitical and economic factors, the Company is expected to maintain its growth in 2025. 
With the existing capacity of its three plants, A, B, and C, WIN Semi. should be 
well-equipped to meet customer demand throughout the year. With an eye on future market

#### C. Development Strategy

Looking ahead to the development strategy, AI applications in optical communications 
and data centers are emerging as key focal points for industry growth, with companies across 
the supply chain actively positioning themselves to seize new opportunities, and WIN Semi 
is no exception. Whether in traditional optical transceivers or silicon photonics technology, 
optical communication transmission relies on III-V compound semiconductors as the core 
materials for photo diodes (PDs) and laser light sources. At the same time, time-of-flight 
(TOF) laser technology is expanding beyond smartphones and automated guided vehicles 
(AGVs) to humanoid robots and automotive collision-avoidance LiDAR. Compound 
semiconductors offer wavelength solutions ranging from short to long to accommodate 
various distance-sensing requirements. As a member of the Taiwan Silicon Photonics 
Industry Alliance, WIN Semi is one of the few companies globally with mass production 
technology and experience in both optical communications and optical sensing wafer 
foundry services. We are seeing strong demand from Optics customers for outsourced 
manufacturing, and we are currently working closely with multiple clients to address these 
needs.

Additionally, WIN Semi is also optimistic about the development of Wi-Fi 7. In 
particular, with U.S.-based flagship smartphones fully upgraded to Wi-Fi 7 in the second half 
of 2024, this is expected to drive broader adoption among Android devices and even 
upgrades of Wi-Fi routers, presenting a promising outlook for Wi-Fi PA demand. Lastly, the 
Infrastructure business moved past the downturn in the previous year and delivered a 
satisfactory growth in 2024. We believe that Optics Electronics components for data centers, 
satellite communications, and military and commercial radar applications will serve as the 
primary growth drivers for WIN Semi’s Infrastructure business in the post-5G era.

#### Macroeconomic Environment

Looking ahead, the greatest challenges continue to be political and economic 
uncertainties around the world. At the time of writing this report, there are signs of potential 
ceasefires in the Russo-Ukraine war and the Middle East. However, the rise of international 
political isolationism is adding uncertainty to the future of the global supply chain. In 
response to the shifting landscape, WIN Semi. must maintain operational flexibility and 
engage in innovation to survive. Internally, WIN Semi. not only upholds the spirit of 
responsibility and corporate governance that we have long promoted, but also aims to create 
a friendly workplace environment, actively retain talents. Externally, we show our concern 
for society and pursue environmental sustainability. We will continue to promote ESG to 
achieve the goal of sustainable management.

#### 4.1.2. Industry Overview


#### A. Current Status and Development of the Industry


#### 1. Current Status and Development of the GaAs Industry

Semiconductor materials can be categorized into single-element semiconductor and 
compound semiconductor. The former are semiconductors made using element like Silicon 
(Si) and Germanium (Ge). The latter are III-V compound semiconductors such as Gallium 
Arsenide (GaAs), Indium Phosphide (InP), or Gallium Nitride (GaN) made using group-III 
element such as Gallium (Ga), Indium (In), or Aluminum (Al) as well as group-V element 
such as Arsenic (As), phosphorus (P), or nitrogen (N). Compared to the silicon 
semiconductors, GaAs semiconductors have characteristics in high frequency, anti-radiation, 
and high breakdown voltage, and are widely adopted for mainstream commercial optical 
communications, wireless communications, and advanced defense, aviation, and satellite 
applications. The popularity of wireless communication is critical to the success of GaAs 
foundry business model. For example, the key components of radio frequency front-end 
modules in both cellular phones and wireless networks (Wi-Fi) are the Power Amplifier (PA), 
RF Switch, and Low Noise Amplifier (LNA). At present, most of the RF power amplifiers


#### -110-


## Page 115

are made from the material of GaAs. The characteristics of GaAs made it an indispensable 
component for wireless communications, optical communications, and advanced national 
defense, aviation, and satellite industries. GaAs semiconductor has its own foundry 
technologies, design flow and verification model different from silicon semiconductor in 
order to satisfy the rapid development of the wireless communication systems, thus allowing 
GaAs to maintain exclusivity and uniqueness in the field.

Due to the continuous progress of the silicon semiconductor process in recent years, the 
operating frequency and performance of RF CMOS have continued to improve. Most of the 
cellular phone and Wi-Fi RF transceivers are made using RF CMOS now. This is the 
inevitable result of the highly integrated single chip SoC technology development in the 
commercial RF IC industry. Although today’s RF CMOS can work in high operating 
frequencies and can be highly integrated, its inherent physical characteristics, such as low 
breakdown voltage, high power consumption, poor signal isolation, and low output power 
density, make it difficult to compete with GaAs in terms of PA applications. Therefore, when 
the telecom standard evolved from 4G LTE into 5G applications and Wi-Fi 6 evolved to 
Wi-Fi 6E/7, GaAs PA had the irreplaceable physical advantage, specifically high power 
transmission. 5G technology’s data transmission rate are expected to be 100 times faster than 
4G LTE theoretically, GaAs PA is capable to meet the characteristic of 5G high speed data 
transmissions and the ratio for Wi-Fi 6E/7 to adopt GaAs PA will increase significantly due 
to the requirement of wider bandwidth, higher frequency and higher linearity. The 
cost-to-performance gap between GaAs and silicon in power amplifier applications continues 
to widen, not narrow. Therefore, GaAs compound semiconductor components will continue 
to be an important component of the communications market.

AI applications in optical communications and data centers are emerging as key focal 
points for industry growth, with companies across the supply chain actively positioning 
themselves to seize new opportunities. Whether in traditional optical transceivers or silicon 
photonics technology, optical communication transmission relies on III-V compound 
semiconductors as the core materials for photo diodes (PDs) and laser light sources. In 
addition, the laser of III-V semiconductor has the advantage of small size and high 
integration, so its applications have been widely used in both industrial and commercial 
fields especially VCSEL which is the most suitable for mass production. Thus, more and 
more new applications of VCSEL were developed in the fields such as Biometric, AR/VR, 
ADAS, proximity sensor and in-cabinet sensing for automobiles. Furthermore, time-of-flight 
(TOF) laser technology is expanding beyond smartphones and automated guided vehicles 
(AGVs) to humanoid robots. Therefore, compound semiconductors will continue to play an 
important role in communication and optical devices markets


#### 2. Current Status and Outlook of the GaAs Foundry Industry

Similar to the Silicon foundry business model, GaAs foundries require a certain scale of 
investment and long-term development of process technologies. These two factors provide 
IC designers fast, convenient, and relatively low cost processes to realize the products they 
designed. The silicon foundry model was a breakthrough that lifted the control of large IDMs 
over parts and components, and allowed small capital companies with an expertise in IC 
design to survive without making large investments in semiconductor manufacturing, which 
they are not familiar with. This specialization of labor has allowed IC design companies to 
focus on developing new generation ICs. Foundries focus on developing more advanced 
process technologies and providing more complete solutions. This win-win strategy 
successfully took down the monopoly of IDMs from IC design to process technology, and

optical communications and optical sensing wafer foundry services. We are seeing strong

frequency bands are being used. However, the higher frequency bands result in shorter 
wavelength, which leads to a smaller coverage area. If we want to achieve high speeds and 
low latency, we must install more small base stations or femtocells. In addition, many areas 
are still not covered by the network due to geography, climate, insufficient infrastructure, and 
other factors. These issues have made LEO satellites a good solution. After acquiring 
aeronautics industrial quality system certification a few years ago, the Company has become 
an indispensable part of the supply chain. The related products including higher frequency 
and higher power GaN PA, LNA with GaAs pHEMT technology, driver amplifier with GaAs 
HBT technology, and integrated passive devices (IPD). In addition, WIN Semi. is 
continuously adopting new frequency band technologies to meet the needs of terminal 
applications. For example, to meet data transmission requirements between satellites and 
gateways, the Company has expanded beyond the existing Ka- and Ku-bands to include 
V-band and E-band, which present new business opportunities. Revenue from LEO satellite 
applications has grew steady. As more satellites are launched, the Company is optimistic 
about future SATCOM developments and their contributions.

#### D. Retirement System

The Company appropriated pension plan in accordance with the Labor Standard Law every 
year. The Company commissions professional actuaries to calculate pension reserve funds to 
ensure that full coverage of pension is reserved. Pension is calculated based on the employee’s 
years of service and the average salary in the six months prior to retirement. The Company 
contributes pension reserve equivalent to 2% of the employees’ salary each month to a special 
account in Bank of Taiwan. As of December 31, 2024, the fair value of plan assets was 
NT$65,348 thousand. Future amounts of pensions that need to be paid as required by law have 
been included in the pension liability. The balance as at the end of 2024 was NT$58,808 
thousand. In addition, the Company appropriates pension funds equivalent to 6% of the 
employee’s salary to the dedicated personal pension account of the Bureau of Labor Insurance 
based on the Labor Pension Act. The number of years worked by employees hired before July 1, 
2005 shall be reserved. The amount of pension expenses in 2024 totaled NT$121,874 thousand.

We established the “Regulations for Management of the Retirement of Commissioned 
Managers” for commissioned managers to feel secure about their retirement and maintain 
harmonious relationships, which will improve our business performance.
