#### I. Report to Shareholders

FY2024 marked an important milestone in our 36-year history since the Company’s 
establishment. Not only did we achieve record-breaking revenue and net profit, but we also made 
significant progress in our ongoing transformation and journey in innovation. For the first time, 
our server business accounted for over 50% of the Company’s total revenue, such achievement 
signifies that Quanta has not only solidified our long-standing market leadership position in the 
global notebook manufacturing industry, but has also successfully established a strategic foothold 
in the new race of Artificial Intelligence (AI).

Since the founding of Quanta Computer, we have continuously advanced and refined our 
understanding in the field of "Compute" technology. With decades of deep-rooted advancements 
and dedication in technology, we have developed deep understanding and profound insights into 
future technology and market transformations. In recent years, we have been focusing on 
empowering the future through transformation and innovation. We’ve set the Company's mission 
as "We make better computer for better world," pushing the advancement of technology and the 
development of applications. Our mission also serves as the driving force behind Quanta’s 
continuous innovation and business expansion. Now, as a new era of computing unfolds, our 
technical expertise is poised to shine. From computing infrastructure to smart devices (Smart-X), 
Quanta’s development in AI is ready and fully in place, our comprehensive offerings in AI solutions 
span from data centers to end devices. Aside from our strong business resilience and competitive 
strength, Quanta has also realized a significant leap in our financial performance.


#### Quanta’s performance in FY2024 and outlook for FY2025 are as follows:


#### Abstract of Quanta’s performance in FY2024

Benefiting from the increased global investments in AI data center infrastructure buildout, 
our server business experienced exponential growth with server revenue more than doubling in 
FY2024. Total notebook shipment delivered in FY2024 was 45.9 million units, a 2.1% year-over-
year decline. Reported consolidated revenue for the year reached NT$1.41 trillion, a 30% increase 
from NT$1.09 trillion reported in the previous year. The reported gross profit margin, operating 
profit margin, and net profit margin in FY2024 were 7.9%, 4.4%, and 4.2% respectively, compared 
to 7.8%, 4.0%, and 3.7% reported in FY2023. Our financial performance achieved another 
remarkable milestone of year-over-year growth across all three profitability metrics.

FY2024 reported gross profit surpassed NT$100 billion for the first time, reaching 
NT$110.76 billion, marking a 30.5% increase from NT$84.89 billion reported in FY2023. Net 
income after tax in the past two years was NT$60.28 billion and NT$40.49 billion, respectively. 
Net profit attributable to owners of parent company was NT$59.70 billion in FY2024 and 
NT$39.68 billion in FY2023, reflecting a year-over-year growth of 50.5%. Total non-operating 
income amounted to approximately NT$11.54 billion. EPS for FY2024 was NT$15.49 and the Board 
of Directors also resolved to distribute cash dividend of NT$13.0 per share, representing 
approximately 83.9% of dividend payout ratio.

#### Operational outlook for FY2025

The emergence of DeepSeek has demonstrated the vast possibilities for diversification and 
innovative applications using Large Language Model (LLM) technology. Furthermore, the 
intensified competition within the AI industry to build infrastructure and computing power, 
alongside the significant reduction in training costs, is simultaneously driving a surge in the 
development of end-user applications. Looking into FY2025, Quanta will continue to center 
around AI to drive the Company’s overall growth. We have already developed a variety of "Smart-
X" intelligent tools with focuses on three major AI application domains, including "Smart 
Manufacturing", "Smart Mobility", and "Smart Medicine". Developing smart solutions that are 
user-centric and humanized, offering optimized solutions that are tailored to different use 
scenarios.

The global geopolitical and economic landscape continue to exhibit turbulence, marked by 
persistent trade disputes and tariff uncertainties. The rapid iteration of new technologies is also 
intensifying global market competition, making today’s business environment more challenging 
and unpredictable than ever. We must navigate these complexities and remain vigilant, agile, and 
ready to adapt the ever-evolving circumstances. To maintain our leadership in the fast-changing 
technology industry, and to seek the long-term sustainable growth of the Company, Quanta will 
advance with three key development strategies in parallel to strengthen our corporate 
foundation:

⚫ 
Advanced Research: innovation is the lifeblood of a technology company. At Quanta, we 
keep an open mindset, embrace our curiosity and passion for learning, while also doubling 
down on our commitment to advanced technology research. We continue to explore new 
possibilities, leveraging our flexibility and strengths to help customers shorten the time from 
technology innovation to market commercialization, so that we can truly realize "Time to 
Technology" when opportunities arise.

⚫ 
Precise Marketing: By deepening our trend analysis of target market and combining with 
our expertise and value propositions, we can precisely position our market segmentation 
and formulate entry strategies. At the same time, we focus on customer segments with the 
greatest growth potential to maximize the return of our investments. Starting with a 
thorough understanding of customer needs, we deliver solutions that are customized, 
differentiated, and optimized to assist our customers to succeed in their perspective 
markets in a timely manner and grow alongside them—"Time to Market."

⚫ 
Manufacturing Resilience: Efficient and high-quality manufacturing have always been the 
resilient footing that Quanta relies on to swiftly realize R&D achievements into the scale 
benefits of "Time to Volume", and to achieve the commercialization of product technologies 
to capture market opportunities. In response to potential tariff disputes, to better serve 
customers locally, and to diversify risks associated with product origins, Quanta has 
expanded our manufacturing footprint beyond China and Taiwan to include production sites 
in the U.S., Germany, Thailand, Vietnam, and Mexico. We have established global 
manufacturing service capabilities that are resilient and competitive through fully optimized 
supply chain management, smart manufacturing, and advanced manufacturing efficiency 
and quality.

components within server and notebooks. The performance of CPU and GPU would directly

particularly critical in domains such as High-Performance Computing (HPC), Machine Learning 
(ML), and Deep Learning (DL), where processing speed and computational power are 
paramount considerations.

data processing, real-time data storage, and data retrieval within cloud computing 
environments. Furthermore, components such as network switches, storage devices, and AI 
accelerator cards would also significantly influence overall system performance and stability, 
substantially enhancing data transmission efficiency and computing power. 
 
Key customers to Quanta are major global PC brands and large-scale CSPs (Cloud Service 
Providers), all of whom maintain stringent requirements for hardware performance. Our 
customers typically possess self-procurement or supply chain management expertise and 
they can independently source related components. Notwithstanding this, Quanta has 
established strong manufacturing capabilities and economies of scale in notebook and cloud 
segments. We also maintain procurement advantages and actively cultivate diversified 
sources for components to mitigate over-reliance on single suppliers. This strategy serves to 
reduce supply chain vulnerabilities, ensure consistent quality of supply, and facilitate the 
production of high-performance products with cost competitiveness.
