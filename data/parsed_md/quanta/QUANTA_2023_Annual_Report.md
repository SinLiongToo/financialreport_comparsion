#### I. Report to Shareholders

The wave of Generative AI has swept crossed the globe at unprecedented speed in 2023, 
and rapidly became a driving force of industry transformation and economic recovery in many 
countries post the pandemics. Quanta thrived and seized the opportunity to capture fruitful 
rewards and delivered another record-high profits for the year. In 2023, the reported profit for 
the year surpassed the Company’s paid-in capital, while market capitalization more than tripled. 
We would like to extend our most sincere gratitude to all shareholders of Quanta for their 
steadfast trust and supports, urging Quanta to adhere to remain true to the original intention of 
an engineer to seek truth and pragmatism. We have long been devoting R&D resources to the 
design of high-performance computing solutions and providing a variety "Tools for SMART X". 
After a long period of hard work accumulation and dedication, we have finally ushered in a new 
era of computing.

The high interest rates and lifted inflation environment have had significant impacts on 
global business operations. According to the TWSE (Taiwan Stock Exchange), both revenue and 
profits reported by all listed companies in Taiwan declined in 2023. Yet, employees of Quanta 
worked diligently and dedicated ourselves with strong commitments in improving efficiency, 
reducing costs, and strengthening the Company's competitiveness in response to market 
dynamics. As a result, Quanta delivered outstanding financial results despite the challenges we 
faced. From AI servers, Smart Mobility, to other product categories, Quanta have expanded 
profitability through increased value-adds, optimized business portfolios, and cost control. We 
continue to enhance the Company's value through innovation and continuous improvement.


#### Quanta’s performance in FY2023 and outlook for FY2024 are as follows:


#### Abstract of Quanta’s performance in FY2023

In FY2023, total notebook shipment delivered was 46.9 million units, securing our 
dominant position in the notebook market. At the same time, we seized the rising opportunities 
in AI server business, while the Smart Mobility business also gained traction among branded auto 
makers in Europe, the U.S., and various regions in Asia. Quanta has taken steady steps in laying a 
solid foundation for success. In response to future business growth in coming years, and to 
provide services in proximity, as well as to diversify the origin risks, the Company has recently 
announced expansion plans for several overseas locations outside China and Taiwan, including 
increased capacity in the U.S., Mexico, Thailand, Vietnam, and Germany, etc. The newly added 
capacity would further enhance Quanta’s global manufacturing and service capabilities.

FY2023 reported consolidated revenue was NT$1.09 trillion. Although the reported 
revenue was a decrease compared to the previous year's revenue of NT$1.28 trillion, the 
Company still delivered improvements in gross margin, operating margin, and net profit margin 
through product mix enhancement and stringent cost control. The reported gross profit margin, 
operating profit margin, and net profit margin in FY2023 were 7.8%, 4.0%, and 3.7% respectively, 
which showed significant improvement compared to 5.5%, 2.4%, and 2.3% reported in FY2022.

FY2023 reported gross profit reached record high of NT$84.89 billion, representing a year-
over-year growth of 19.7% compared to NT$70.91 billion reported in FY2022. Net income after 
tax in the past two years was NT$40.49 billion and NT$29.72 billion, respectively. Net profit 
attributable to owners of parent company was NT$39.68 billion in FY2023 and NT$28.96 billion in 
FY2022, representing a year-on-year increase of 37.0%. EPS for FY2023 was NT$10.29 and the 
Board of Directors also resolved the cash dividend distribution plan of NT$9.0 per share, which

#### Operational outlook for FY2024

Technology advancement happens rapidly and market dynamics are difficult to predict, 
Quanta has taken the lead in the first wave of infrastructure build out for generative AI. Our 
product offerings in 3A categories, including AI PCs, AI servers, and AI car computer, are firmly 
established. As AI applications and services become widely available, we aim to capitalize our 3A 
product categories with the rise of AI revolution to propel Quanta forward in becoming an "A+" 
company.

The technology industry has gone through several cycles of changes. Drawing from past 
experiences, the key success factors in adopting new technologies depend not only on the 
technology itself, but also on changes in user behavior, as well as the formation of new business 
models. We must continuously monitor and observe the trends of innovation, and incorporate 
the AI technology in the three major fields of "SMART X", including "Smart Medicine, Smart 
Manufacturing, and Smart Mobility." We shall provide the most appropriate tools in SMART X to 
assist our clients in developing high-quality services and solutions. Doing so would enable Quanta 
to seize the moment of business opportunities precisely when applications of new technology 
arise.

In Smart Medicine, we continuously devote R&D resources into developing collaborative 
applications and tools to improve the quality of medical care and smart solutions to enhance the 
accessibility of medical resources. These efforts are aimed to improve the efficiency of healthcare 
and to facilitate cross-sector and cross-border collaborations. We integrate AI technology with 
telemedicine, smart health monitoring, and related medical devices, in providing more precise 
smart medical diagnosis and treatment solutions. Quanta aims to contribute in achieving a future 
of healthcare that is more efficient, convenient, precise, and humanized.

In Smart Manufacturing, the transition from traditional computer vision inspection to 
visual inspection with AI technology implies the development of Large Language Model (LLM) will 
once again bring new possibilities for the manufacturing industry. Embedding AI technology in 
testing, validation, and optimization of processes and products through different product 
lifecycles, will reduce the reliance on human operations and minimize mistakes. We aim to 
develop new applications in smart manufacturing, leveraging our expertise in wireless 
communication, cloud computing, and generative AI.

In Smart Mobility, we are committed to advancing the intelligence, efficiency, and 
environmental friendliness of transportation systems. We apply Quanta's R&D expertise in high-
speed computing to the next generation of central computer ECUs and domain control units in 
autonomous driving technology. Harnessing our advantages in technology, we aim to assist 
customers in integrating AI and sensor technologies to increase the safety and convenience of 
driving experiences.

The ongoing geopolitical conflicts, technology rivalry between China and the U.S., coupled 
with inflation and stagnant consumer spending, continue to pose challenges for the global 
economy. However, people's desire to pursuit and to explore happiness through new value 
propositions, lifestyles, and work patterns has never waned, such desire has also infused the 
market with a constant flow of vitality and business opportunities. Quanta is positioned in the

other international environmental regulations and fulfill our corporate social responsibility.

We are committed to environmental protection and devote environmental protection

as a tool for environmental management within the Company. Our goal is to optimize 
environmental management performance and make voluntary and continual improvement, 
in order to improve the environment and reduce environmental impacts. We have established

manufacturing plant establishment. The organization is responsible for improving the EHS 
performance throughout the Company; plan, promote, and implement organizational EHS

Company’s EHS performance. Through the collaboration of the EHS committee from each 
department, we build a well-established EHS management system.

5.4.3  Water and Waste Management 
5.4.3.1 Water Management

Tap water is the main water source for production sites in Taiwan, including QRDC and QTMC 
manufacturing sites, and is used primarily for general daily activities, air conditioning, and 
kitchen usage. In 2023, the total water consumption amounted to 418,114 metric tons. 
 
To conserve water resources and to reduce consumption, relevant department has been 
reviewing water-conservation solutions and water usage equipment annually. Measures 
implemented to date include, installation of sensor-operated faucets with water-saving 
controls to regulate water flow in restrooms at QRDC, installation of rainfall sensors in the 
peripheral green belt irrigation system to prevent unnecessary water waste. QTMC QC3 is a 
newly established building with rainfall harvesting system installed and the capacity of rainfall 
recycle is 244.7 cubic meters. 
 
Most of the wastewater generated from each site in the Hwaya Technology Park is from 
general daily activities and kitchen usage, not from manufacturing processes, and the 
wastewater management is handled by the Park Management Center's sewage treatment 
plant with a discharge volume of 177,651 metric tons. Quanta has taken necessary steps to 
prevent the wastewater from contaminating the environment and equip each site with 
primary wastewater treatment facilities. Designated personnel is responsible for regular 
inspections and maintenance, ensuring the wastewater treatment facilities operate normally 
and that the discharged water quality meets the qualifying standards. Quanta also entrust 
qualified verification companies to conduct testing semi-annually. 
 
5.4.3.2. Waste Management

There are three major types of industrial waste generated by Quanta’s businesses, including 
general industrial waste, hazardous industrial waste, and resource waste. The weights of 
these types are 1,223 metric tons, 311 metric tons, and 5,165 metric tons, respectively. In 
order to manage those waste effectively and to achieve the goal of waste reduction, we 
ensure to comply with applicable regulations in the transportation, treatment, and recycling 
processes of those waste. We thus entrust qualified companies to handle waste management 
and keep records. Relevant department would conduct random audits on the processing 
vendors to ensure they operate in compliance with relevant laws and regulations. 
 
We have taken one step further to implement effective management that exceeds legal 
requirements by gradually introducing the "Zero Waste to Landfill Program" at each site, 
adopting UL Zero Waste to Landfill Verification (UL ECVP 2799). The verification requires 
compliance management, inspection, and auditing of all waste flows within the company, 
ensuring that waste undergoes proper processes such as recycling, reuse, and transformation 
instead of direct landfill disposal. Verification can only be obtained when overall waste 
conversion rate reaches 80% or higher. QTMC QC1 has obtained the platinum-level 
certification in 2022, marking a milestone towards resource sustainability.
