- **Revenue**: $77,867.0M
- **Gross Profit**: $43,806.0M (Gross Margin: 56.26%)
- **Operating Income**: $22,083.0M (Operating Margin: 28.36%)
- **Net Income**: $20,899.0M
- **R&D Expenses**: $13,556.0M (17.41% of Rev)
- **Global Headcount**: 110,600 FTEs
- **Rev per FTE**: $704,042
- **GP per FTE**: $396,076

## Strategic Insights & Lean Maturity

- **The Pivot**: Intel's structural pivot centers on separated internal foundry accounting, 18A node execution, and aggressive workforce streamlining from 131.9k to ~102k FTEs.
- **Lean Maturity**: Level 4 (IDM 2.0 Internal Foundry Architecture & High-NA EUV Manufacturing)
