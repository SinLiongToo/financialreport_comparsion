#### United Microelectronics Corporation  |  Annual Report 2024


#### Dear Valued Shareholders,


#### The year 2024 was marked by challenges despite the end of the

COVID-19 pandemic and a gradually recovering global economy.


#### The semiconductor industry navigated a complex landscape amid

geopolitical tensions, inflation, inventory adjustments, and overcapacity.


#### In this environment, UMC demonstrated resilience with stable profitability


#### and made important strides in technology advancement to position the

Company to capture future growth.


#### For UMC, 2024 was a critical year for strategic planning, with significant


#### progress made in technology development. The collaboration with Intel


#### to jointly develop a 12nm platform is on track, which extends technology


#### migrations for our customers while solidifying our leadership in specialty


#### processes. To tap into opportunities in the era of AI and edge computing,

we are also actively developing advanced packaging solutions.


#### UMC has long been committed to a diversified manufacturing footprint,


#### which has become a key differentiator. Production facilities across


#### Taiwan, Singapore, Japan, and China enable customers to bolster their


#### supply chain resilience. With the new expansion at our Singapore Fab


#### 12i, coupled with the 12nm collaboration, which will be manufactured at


#### our partner’s U.S. site, we will be able to provide customers with greater

flexibility amid a rapidly changing geopolitical landscape.


#### We also continued to invest in digital transformation and corporate


#### sustainability. Our flagship Fab 12A’s integration of Industry 4.0


#### solutions to enhance efficiency and quality has been recognized as


#### a smart manufacturing Global Lighthouse by the World Economic


#### Forum. In pursuit of our net zero goal, we continued to drive reduction


#### in greenhouse gas emissions and energy consumption, while actively


#### sourcing renewable energy to meet our targets of 50% renewable energy

use by 2030 and 100% by 2050.


#### 2024 Business Overview


#### In 2024, UMC shipped approximately 3.446 million equivalent 12-inch


#### wafers, with annual revenue reaching NT$232.3 billion. Gross margin


#### for the year was 32.6% and operating margin was 22.2%. Net profit


#### attributable to the parent company amounted to NT$47.2 billion, with


#### earnings per share of NT$3.80. Capital expenditures were approximately


#### US$2.9 billion, primarily allocated for capacity expansions in Tainan,


#### Taiwan; Singapore; and Xiamen, China; and the rest for product mix


#### optimization. These expansion projects will fuel growth in long-term

profitability and market share.


#### Technology Leadership and Development


#### In 2024, UMC invested NT$15.6 billion in research and development,


#### focusing on process technologies required for 5G communication,

artificial intelligence, the Internet of Things, and automotive electronics.


#### With the advancement in process manufacturing, UMC’s development


#### of 12nm and 14nm specialty processes  and advanced packaging has


#### become crucial to maintaining leadership and achieving technology


#### differentiation. Below is an overview of key R&D projects:


#### 1. 12nm FinFET Process Technology Platform (12FFC): This platform


#### offers smaller chip area and lower power consumption compared


#### to the 14FFC platform, significantly enhancing performance while


#### fully leveraging the advantages of FinFET technology. It is widely


#### applicable to various semiconductor products. UMC’s collaboration


#### with Intel in the U.S. is a key strategy for pursuing cost-effective


#### capacity expansion and technology upgrades. According to customers’


#### feedback, 12FFC’s performance is highly competitive and customers


#### are optimistic about the business opportunities. This partnership

#### will assist customers in upgrading to critical technology nodes and

enhancing supply chain resilience with a North American presence.


#### Process development is expected to be completed and validated by

2026.


#### 2. 14nm FinFET Embedded High Voltage Process Technology Platform


#### (14eHV): This platform significantly improves chip performance


#### compared to the 28eHV platform, offering smaller chip area and


#### reduction in energy consumption by over 40%. Its key application is


#### for display driver chips, providing higher display resolution and refresh

rates.


#### 3. 22nm Image Signal Processor (22ISP): Mass production of 22ISP


#### began in 2024, becoming the first product for customers utilizing the


#### 22nm process.  It not only enhances performance but also significantly


#### reduces power consumption, providing customers with better product

competitiveness.


#### 4. 28nm Embedded High Voltage Low Power Process Platform


#### (28eHV-LP): This platform was completed in 2024 and is suitable


#### for AMOLED panel display driver ICs, achieving a 15% reduction in


#### energy consumption without compromising image quality or data

rates. Mass production is expected to commence in 2025.


#### 5. 28nm Non-Volatile Memory Process Platform (28ESF4) and 22nm


#### Resistive Memory Process Platform (22RRAM): Both platforms have

completed validation for general-purpose and IoT microprocessors.


#### The 28ESF4 has been optimized to meet automotive chip specifications,

making it suitable for the automotive electronics market.


#### 6. 110nm BCD 3.3V New Application Platform: This platform is currently


#### entering the design finalization and validation stage, with mass

production expected in 2025.


#### 7. 153nm Customized Mobile Power IC (PMIC): A successful trial

production took place in 2024, with mass production expected in 2025.


#### 8. Advanced Packaging Technology: In mobile device radio frequency


#### (RF) components, wafer-to-wafer (W2W) hybrid bonding technology


#### has been developed to achieve size reduction, with product validation


#### completed and mass production expected to start in 2025. For edge


#### AI applications, UMC is collaborating with supply chain partners to


#### offer W2W solutions for the heterogeneous integration of logic chips


#### and customized memory. For high-performance computing (HPC)


#### and cloud AI applications, deep trench capacitors (DTC) have been


#### successfully integrated into silicon interposers, providing excellent


#### power integrity for 2.5D packaged chips; product validation has been

completed and trial production is underway.


#### 9. Gallium Nitride (GaN) Device Processes: Successful development


#### of GaN RF device processes and GaN power device processes has


#### been completed, with GaN RF switch device processes entering


#### mass production. The GaN RF power amplifier (PA) device process


#### and GaN 650V power device process are both undergoing customer

product validation and trial production.

#### Intellectual Property and Compliance


#### In the face of intense global technological competition, UMC is dedicated


#### to protecting its R&D achievements and the value created with partners


#### through a dual-track protection mechanism of patents and trade secrets,


#### enhancing its competitiveness. Currently, UMC has over 15,500 patents,

providing a solid intellectual property barrier for its process technologies.


#### Legal compliance is the cornerstone of business operations. UMC is


#### committed to adhering to laws and regulations and has established a


#### comprehensive compliance system. Regular legal training is conducted


#### to ensure employees follow relevant requirements. UMC actively


#### monitors legal changes and adjusts internal policies to maintain legality


#### and compliance, achieving sustainable development while earning trust

and support from all stakeholders.


#### Sustainability


#### Sustainability is a key priority at UMC, deeply integrated into our


#### corporate culture. To accelerate our transition to net zero emissions,


#### UMC has signed the Science-Based Targets (SBT) commitment, raising


#### our greenhouse gas emissions reduction target for 2030 to 42% to align


#### with the Paris Agreement’s goal of limiting global warming to 1.5°C. In


#### 2024, UMC implemented an internal carbon price of US$100 per ton


#### to incentivize internal initiatives for carbon reduction. Our adoption of


#### renewable energy as a group reached 16% of total energy consumption


#### in 2024. In December, we signed an offshore wind power purchase


#### agreement for more than 30 billion kilowatt-hours of power, putting us


#### well on our way to achieve the interim goal of 50% renewable energy use

by 2030.


#### In 2024, we established a women’s resource group as part of our

commitment to provide a diverse and inclusive workplace environment.


#### To encourage employees to give back to our communities, we rolled out


#### a program to motivate volunteer participation. UMC’s board of directors


#### is highly diverse and independent, with independent directors holding


#### two-thirds of the seats and female directors constituting one-third. We


#### also implemented a “Manager Stock Ownership Policy” to strengthen the


#### connection between management’s and shareholders’ interests, as well


#### as linked employee compensation with ESG performance to promote

sustainable development.


#### Honors and Awards


#### In 2024, UMC was recognized as a sustainability leader in the

semiconductor industry by the Dow Jones Sustainability Indices (DJSI).


#### The Company has received numerous honors and accolades from

#### Outlook


#### Semiconductors are the cornerstone of the global economy and will


#### play an increasingly critical role in driving our society forward. UMC is


#### confident about the prospects of the semiconductor industry and our


#### company’s development. As a leader in global wafer foundry services,


#### UMC has earned deep trust from our customers through 45 years of


#### manufacturing experience, comprehensive technology offering, and


#### an efficient and diversified operational model. We will keep pursuing


#### technology innovation to meet the dynamic needs of our customers and

to set new benchmarks in the industry.


#### Looking ahead, while geopolitical and overcapacity concerns persist,


#### the uneven inventory adjustments across end markets is expected


#### to gradually improve while AI-related applications will bring new


#### opportunities. UMC will continue to build upon our foundation, focusing


#### on technology R&D, diversified capacity, excellence in manufacturing,


#### and advancement towards our net zero goal. In doing so, we strive to

provide stable returns and long-term growth for our shareholders.


#### Finally, we would like to express our sincere gratitude to all our


#### shareholders for your support of UMC. We look forward to sharing more


#### of UMC’s progress and achievements with you in the coming year as we

work together to create a brighter future.

UMC has established the Policy for Nomination and Election of Directors that takes into account the Company’s organizational culture, business model and long-term development, and

also established criteria to ensure the diversity of the Board members. There are currently three seats reserved for female directors. Based on our policy of gender equality, UMC will

years and two directors who were newly elected on May 30, 2024. The board of UMC is composed of members with diversified backgrounds of industry, government and academia. The

experiences of Board members include the president of a national university, a fellow of Academia Sinica, the Minister of Science and Technology, the president of ITRI, financial experts

Risks to the Company's Business due 
to China’s Development of Domestic 
Semiconductor Manufacturing Capabilities, 
and the Countermeasures

China has been developing semiconductor supply chain autonomy. Due 
to restrictions on the import of advanced technologies and equipment, 
China turns to massive expansion of mature node production capacity 
and actively subsidizes domestic manufacturers. Semiconductor 
manufacturing competitors in China have been able to reduce price to 
expand their market share with the support of government subsidies. It is 
estimated that as China semiconductor capacity continues to grow, the 
competitive situation will be more exacerbated. This may have an impact 
on UMC's orders, revenue and profits. For example:
1. China’s large-scale expansion overlaps with UMC's production 
capacity. Moreover, Chinese semiconductor manufacturers may 
take advantage of government subsidies to trigger aggressive 
price competition to win customers, especially Chinese customers 
or customers serving the Chinese market, resulting in a decline in 
UMC’s revenue and profits. 
2. Policy subsidies for China’s IC design industry may impact the 
market share of other IC design companies, resulting in the loss of 
customer orders related to Taiwan’s semiconductor foundries. 
3. Numerous emerging small and medium-sized semiconductor 
foundries in China, each focusing on the development of specific 
technologies, may challenge UMC’s leading position in mature 
process technologies, leading to the loss of business.

UMC’s countermeasures include:
1. Analyzing future market opportunities, improving customer demand 
management, proactively establishing technological differentiation, 
and accelerating FinFET technology development to raise the 
barriers to technology competition.
2. Promoting structural cost improvements and productivity enhancements 
to maintain cost competitiveness.
3. Fully utilizing the advantages of UMC HJ and USCXM to actively 
serve Chinese customers, as well as global customers planning 
to enter the Chinese market, and to create opportunities for 
collaboration.
4. Continuously following up on industry policies in China and actively 
seeking to communicate with the government to obtain relevant 
subsidies.
5. To respond to the competition in different market segments, the 
Company adopts more diversified and flexible pricing strategies to 
win customer orders.

The Impact of Infectious Diseases on the 
Company’s Operations and Countermeasures

Any outbreak of infectious diseases, such as coronavirus (COVID-19), 
Ebola, bird flu, swine flu, or severe acute respiratory syndrome (SARS), 
can affect employee attendance, and even affect company operations. For

affected the global economy and disrupted supply chain. It may expose 
UMC to certain risks, including: unstable sales activities and customer 
orders, reduced plant operations and manpower, international travel 
and communication difficulties, limitations for transnational cooperation 
projects, and disruption to supply of materials.

various epidemic diseases and tracks their dynamic changes to respond 
in a timely manner. For example, UMC established the coronavirus 
epidemic control and response center in January 2020 to coordinate 
relevant information and resources, including:

1. Regularly review and discuss issues including the impact on production, 
supply chain management, epidemic prevention strategies, and 
response measures.

management, develop response measures for each stage, and take 
actions according to the epidemic situation. For example: minimize 
employee infection and production impact; increase buffer inventory, 
and find and expedite the verification of alternative suppliers to 
reduce the risks of material shortages; and lessen the operational 
impact of specific fabs by adjusting or transferring the allocation of 
orders to other facilities. 
3. In order to respond to relevant risks and opportunities, the Company 
closely monitors the medium- to long-term developments in the 
industry, such as inventory adjustments, structural changes in 
market demand, and restructuring of the global supply chain.

domestic and international public health-related information, and 
maintain a consistent cautious attitude and agile response to reduce the 
impact of any epidemic infectious diseases on the Company.

To fulfill corporate social responsibility and facilitate economic, 
environmental and social progress for the purpose of achieving 
sustainable development, the Company has formulated and complies 
with UMC Sustainable Development Best Practice Principles. 
Through the Corporate Sustainability Committee, UMC stays up to 
date on the development of domestic and international sustainable 
development standards and changes in the corporate environment. UMC 
regularly reviews and improves its sustainable development practices, 
systems and management policies to improve the effectiveness of its 
sustainable development implementations. For detailed sustainable 
governance structure and function, please refer to the Company’s 
website at https://www.umc.com/en/Html/esg-governance.
As of 2024, UMC has been listed as one of the Dow Jones Sustainability 
World Index for 17 consecutive years. DJSI is one of the most credible 
international corporate sustainability evaluation tools, so this is an 
indication that UMC’s sustainability performance has been recognized 
by the international investor sector as a global leading enterprise. 
The sustainable awards granted to UMC in 2024 are listed below:
•  Selected as a member of Dow Jones Sustainability Indices (DJSI) 
World Index for the seventeenth straight year and also named a 
constituent of Emerging Markets Index; 
•  Rated at “AA” in MSCI ESG Ratings for the second consecutive year 
and selected as component of MSCI ACWI ESG Leaders Index and 
MSCI Emerging Markets ESG Leaders Index;
•  Selected as component of FTSE4Good All-World Index and FTSE4Good 
Emerging Index for the tenth consecutive year, and FTSE4Good TIP 
Taiwan ESG Index since the launch of the index in 2017;
•  Rated “Prime” by ISS ESG Corporate Rating for the eighth consecutive year;
•  Received a rating of “low risk” from the Sustainalytics ESG Risk Rating;
•  Recognized double A list by CDP for Climate Change and Water 
Security for three consecutive years;
•  Awarded TCSA “Corporate Sustainability Report Award” and the 
highest Platinum award for the seventh time; 
•  Awarded “Top 10 Taiwanese Companies Sustainability Model Award” 
for the sixth time; 
These honors and recognitions indicate that, after long-term dedication 
to sustainability, UMC’s actions, transparency and completeness of 
information disclosure have all reached the international level, such that 
it has become a global benchmark enterprise.
In addition to continuous dedication to this industry, the self-expectation 
of UMC is to become a green giant within the semiconductor industry. 
Energy saving and carbon reduction is a long-term undertaking, in 
which UMC is an advocator of low carbon innovation and provider of 
low carbon solution as well as an implementer of low carbon actions. 
UMC established the Fluorinated Greenhouse Gases Reduction Work 
Group as early as 1999 in response to global warming, and it achieved 
its 2010 voluntary reduction goal outlined by Taiwan Semiconductor 
Industry Association ahead of schedule. On Earth Day (April 22) in 
2010, UMC was the first in the industry to announce a “Climate Change 
Policy” and “UMC’s Low Carbon Commitment” as the highest guidelines 
in response to climate change. In 2021, UMC is ardently responding to 
the Paris Climate Agreement and announced its pledge to reach net

zero emissions by 2050. In the meantime, UMC become the second 
semiconductor foundry to join RE100, the global corporate renewable 
energy initiative, and committed to use 100% renewable energy by 2050 
by setting progressive goals of 25% by 2025 and 50% by 2030.

In 2013, UMC started an early reduction project for carbon reduction 
allowance approved by the EPA, which was based on the Company’s 
carbon reduction achievements for more than a decade. UMC completed 
the first carbon trade recognized by Taiwan’s EPA in 2014, which 
can be regarded as an important milestone for the domestic carbon 
trading market. The income from this carbon trade was used for the 
environmental protection fund and to launch the “UMC Eco-Echo 
Ecology Preservation Hope Project”. In 2016, UMC extended the spirit 
of this project and launched the UMC Eco Echo Award. It has collected 
outstanding projects and creative ideas for a friendly environment via 
the execution and selection of the Eco Echo Award in order to urge more 
people to join this environmental protection effort. By 2024, more and 
more enterprises were participating in the care for local Taiwan ecology; 
in terms of society, UMC also provides disadvantaged organizations 
with energy saving and carbon reduction services through its energy 
saving service team in the three aspects of energy conservation and 
safety counseling, technical information on energy and resources, and 
engineering improvement in order to fully utilize corporate resources in 
response to global warming.

UMC has also actively promoted clean production and green 
product management. In 2006, the Company was the first among 
all semiconductor makers in the world to complete the third party 
verification audit of Hazardous Substance Process Management (HSPM) 
system, and UMC became the first company in the world to acquire 
QC-080000 IECQ HSPM certification for all fabs. In 2009, it completed 
the first “product carbon footprint” verification of IC wafers according 
to the International Carbon Footprint Standard PAS2050. In 2010, 
UMC completed the “product water footprint” verification of IC wafers 
according to the Business Water Footprint Accounting criteria of the 
non-profit international organization “Water Footprint Network.” As an 
important milestone for development of green production, these results 
can also provide complete, scientific, and reliable product information as 
the basis for self-review and continuous improvement. In 2017, UMC took 
the initiative to launch the Triple R Major League project. UMC expects 
to cooperate with suppliers to promote reuse, recycling and reduction 
measures to provide green products with lower environmental impact. 
In 2022, UMC has launched the Supply Chain Greenhouse Gas (GHG) 
Inventory Initiative. By providing tools and resources for measuring and 
managing emissions, UMC aims to assist 500 suppliers in completing 
their GHG inventories, striving towards its goal of 20% reduction in 
supply chain emissions by 2030. On the other hand, UMC has also 
established a supplier management strategy to strengthen the economic, 
social and environmental performance by implementing supplier 
sustainability assessments, on-site audits, and coaching improvements 
and tracking to create a positive influence and enhance sustainable 
competitiveness of the overall supply chain.
