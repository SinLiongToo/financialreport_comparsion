#### Dear valued shareholders,


#### Before anything else, we would like to thank you for your support of


#### UMC throughout 2023, which was a year filled with many external


#### disturbances. The over macro environment has been significantly


#### affected by global inflationary pressure, high interest rates, and


#### geopolitical tensions, which led to the soaring prices of raw materials


#### and the sharp decline in the demand for consumer electronics. These


#### circumstances have led to a slower than expected inventory depletion in


#### the semiconductor market. To tackle these challenges, UMC diversified


#### offering by introducing new specialty process technologies and by


#### optimizing our product portfolio. As a result, UMC was able to maintain a


#### solid structural profitability despite the weak demand for semiconductor


#### that caused the decline of our capacity utilization rate. UMC’s strong


#### financial resilience is attributed to the Company’s diversified and


#### quality customer base, as well as revenue contribution from specialty

technologies.


#### UMC will bolster our cooperation with industry-leading companies to


#### develop next-generation products through diversified manufacturing


#### locations and differentiated 12-inch specialty processes, and strengthen


#### strategic partnership with key customers. The Company will continue


#### to navigate industry dynamics and rising geopolitical tensions by

strengthening our business and provide stable shareholder returns.


#### 2023 Business Overview


#### In 2023, UMC’s annual wafer shipments reached 3.195 million 12-


#### inch equivalent wafers, with annual revenue of NT$222.5 billion, gross


#### margin of 34.9%, operating margin of 26.0%, and net profit attributable


#### to shareholders of the parent company of NT$61.0 billion. Earnings


#### per share were NT$4.93. Capital expenditures were approximately


#### US$3 billion, including the capacity expansion at Fab 12A in Southern


#### Taiwan Science Park, initial greenfield construction at Fab 12i Phase


#### 3 in Singapore as well as product portfolio optimization efforts. These


#### CAPEX objectives will help UMC achieve steady growth in the long-term

profitability and increase market share.


#### Technology Leadership and Development


#### In 2023, UMC invested NT$13.3 billion in research and development to


#### develop process technologies required for 5G, AI, IoT, and automotive


#### electronics. The following are key achievements:


#### 1.	 Completed the development of the 28nm embedded high-voltage


#### low-power (28eHV-LP) platform for AMOLED panel display driver ICs


#### at lower power consumption compared to 28eHV process. UMC is the


#### first company in the industry to complete the development of the 22nm


#### embedded high-voltage (22eHV) process technology which has been

qualified by customers and has entered volume production.


#### 2.	 The 22nm image signal processor (22ISP) technology, currently


#### under pilot production, provides a superior performance at lower power

consumption compared to the 28nm ISP process platform.


#### 3.	 The Company also launched the 40nm RFSOI process platform


#### supporting millimeter wave (mmWave) radio frequency (RF) front-end


#### chips. The 40RFSOI platform integrates beamformer, core and passive


#### components and front-end components into a single RF chip designed

into mobile, Wi-Fi, automotive, IoT and satellite communications.

40RFSOI is expected to enter mass production in 2024.


#### 4.	 The non-volatile memory (eNVM) process platform provides eNVM

specialty process platform ranging from 180nm to 22nm technologies.


#### The eNVM are well-suited for IoT, automotive, industrial control. The

#### Intellectual Property


#### While preserving the Company’s Intellectual Property amid changing


#### market dynamics, UMC strives to protect and leverage our IP assets by


#### linking our patents with the Company’s business strategy to enhance


#### business competitiveness. The total number of patents granted each year


#### has grown, and the cumulative number of patents granted worldwide has

exceeded 15,000, providing UMC with a comprehensive patent portfolio.


#### In addition, UMC was the only company in 2023 to win the highest honor,


#### a “AAA” rating, from Taiwan Intellectual Property Management System


#### (TIPS) thanks to the Company’s solid intellectual property management

performance.


#### Corporate Sustainability


#### Driven by our sustainability vision, UMC strives to balance business


#### needs with the advancement of our environment, social, and governance


#### (ESG) responsibilities. In 2023, UMC established the "UMC Dr. Liu


#### Jiong-Lang Ph.D. Scholarship" to cultivate outstanding semiconductor


#### talents and to express its gratitude to Academician Liu Jiong-Lang for


#### his long-term dedication to electrical and information education and


#### his guidance and contributions to the Company’s business decisions


#### during his tenure as a UMC director. In addition, UMC announced the


#### establishment of the "Circular Economy Resource Creation Center" to


#### move towards the goal of sustainability and zero waste. After its official


#### opening in 2025, the center is expected to reduce UMC’s waste output


#### by one-third and create green economic benefits of about NT$100


#### million. UMC is also actively responding to emerging ESG trends, such


#### as issuing a diversity and inclusion declaration to establish a diverse


#### and inclusive workplace culture. UMC also announced its commitment


#### to achieving net positive environmental impact and zero deforestation


#### by 2050 to echo the international focus on biodiversity and forest

conservation trends.


#### Honors and Awards


#### In 2023, UMC maintained its leading position in the global wafer foundry


#### industry in the Dow Jones Sustainability Index (DJSI). Moreover, we


#### were honored with numerous awards and recognitions domestically


#### and internationally for our achievements in sustainable development,


#### information disclosure, corporate governance, environmental protection,


#### and customer service. These include CDP’s “double A List” for climate


#### change and water security, “AA” ESG rating by MSCI, and top “5%”


#### ranking in the Taiwan Stock Exchange’s Corporate Governance

Evaluation.

#### Corporate Profile and Date of Establishment


#### Established on May 22, 1980, UMC is Taiwan's first semiconductor


#### company, and has grown to become a leading global semiconductor


#### foundry. The company provides high quality IC production with a focus


#### on both logic and specialty technologies to serve every major sector


#### of the electronics industry. UMC’s comprehensive technology and


#### manufacturing solutions include Logic/Mixed-Signal, embedded High-

Voltage, embedded Non-Volatile-Memory, RFSOI and BCD etc.


#### UMC led the development of the semiconductor industry in Taiwan. It


#### was the first domestic company to offer foundry services, and the first

semiconductor company to list on the Taiwan Stock Exchange (1985).


#### With over 20,000 employees worldwide, UMC is committed to provide


#### customers with robust process technologies and comprehensive foundry


#### solutions, and to enable customers to continuously strengthen their

competitive advantage in today's rapidly changing industry.


#### UMC has 12 wafer fabs located in Asia, four of which are advanced 12-


#### inch fabs. All of UMC’s fabs are certified with IATF 16949 automotive

quality standard.


#### •	 Fab 12A, located in Tainan, Taiwan, has been in volume production


#### since 2002, and is UMC’s advance fab currently manufacturing nodes

down to 14nm.


#### •	 Fab 12i, located in Singapore, which began volume production in


#### 2004, is the company’s specialty technology center that provides


#### highly specialized nodes down to 40nm to serve a diverse range of

applications.


#### •	 Fab 12X (United Semiconductor), located in Xiamen, China, which


#### began volume production in 2016, is southern China's first 12-inch

foundry fab and offers products down to 22nm.


#### •	 Fab 12M, (United Semiconductor Japan Co.) located in Mie, Japan,


#### was fully acquired in October 2019, and offers foundry volume

production for mature specialty nodes down to 40nm.


#### The locations of our fabs offer our customers a geographically diversified


#### and resilient supply chain. In addition, UMC has an extensive network of


#### sales offices in Taiwan, China, Europe, Japan, Korea, Singapore, and

the United States to better serve our customers.


#### UMC, with over four decades of manufacturing expertise, ensures


#### industry-leading cycle times and defect densities. Comprehensive


#### process control systems with advanced methodologies and a strong


#### engineering team support fast product ramp. UMC’s advanced


#### automation, mature defect density, fast cycle times and more than


#### 400,000 wafers per month (12-in equivalent) capacity enable UMC to


#### provide the most competitive manufacturing advantages for our foundry

customers.

#### The Diversity & Independence of the Board of Directors:


#### (1) The Diversity of the Board of Directors:

UMC has established the Policy for Nomination and Election of Directors that takes into account the Company’s organizational culture, business model and long-term development,

and also established criteria to ensure the diversity of the Board members. There are currently two seats reserved for female directors. Based on our policy of gender equality, UMC

will increase the female director seats progressively. The tenures of independent directors are also diversified; there is one director who has served for eight years, two directors for

five years and two directors who were newly elected on July 7, 2021. The board of UMC is composed of members with diversified backgrounds of industry, government and academia.

The experiences of Board members include the president of a national university, a fellow of Academia Sinica, the Minister of Science and Technology, the president of ITRI, financial

experts in finance, accounting and strategy management fields, and professionals from the semiconductor industry and ICT business administration. The directors satisfy basic criteria

and professionalism requirements, and through activities of the sub-committees, can also contribute their experiences to supervise and govern issues related to corporate governance,

environmental sustainability, corporate social responsibility, legal compliance and human rights protection. Please refer to UMC’s Policy for Nomination and Election of Directors for UMC

Board diversification criteria, the Directors’ Information and the Skill Matrix of Board members for implementation of Board diversification on the Company’s website: https://www.umc.

com/en/IR_Director/directors_information.


#### (2) The Independence of the Board of Directors:

The UMC Board of Directors comprises of nine members, including five seats for independent directors and one for a non-executive director; three serve as administrative directors. More

than one half of the director seats are independent directors.

(3) There are two seats reserved for female directors, and more than one half of the director seats are independent directors for the Board. UMC will enhance diversity and independence of

the Board continuously based on the status quo.

affected the global economy and disrupted supply chain. It may expose 
UMC to certain risks, including: unstable sales activities and customer 
orders, reduced plant operations and manpower, international travel 
and communication difficulties, limitations for transnational cooperation 
projects, and disruption to supply of materials.

various epidemic diseases and tracks their dynamic changes to respond 
in a timely manner. For example, UMC established the coronavirus

relevant information and resources, including:

supply chain management, epidemic prevention strategies, and 
response measures.

management, develop response measures for each stage, and take 
actions according to the epidemic situation. For example: minimize 
employee infection and production impact; increase buffer inventory, 
and find and expedite the verification of alternative suppliers to 
reduce the risks of material shortages; and lessen the operational 
impact of specific fabs by adjusting or transferring the allocation of 
orders to other facilities.

closely monitors the medium- to long-term developments in the 
industry, such as inventory adjustments, structural changes in 
market demand, and restructuring of the global supply chain.

domestic and international public health-related information, and 
maintain a consistent cautious attitude and agile response to reduce the 
impact of any epidemic infectious diseases on the Company.
