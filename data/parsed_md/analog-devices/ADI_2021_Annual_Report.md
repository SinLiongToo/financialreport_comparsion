### Strategic Footprint & Human Capital:
Analog Devices bridges the physical and digital domains with industry-leading precision signal processing, power management (following the Maxim Integrated acquisition), RF/microwave subsystems, and sensors. The company operates a high-resilience hybrid Fab-Lite manufacturing model across internal fabs and advanced foundry partnerships.
